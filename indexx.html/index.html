Our Story

Inspired by the Parisian Brasseries

Pastis NYC brings the authentic flavors and ambiance of a traditional Parisian brasserie to the heart of New York City. Founded in 2023, our restaurant pays homage to the classic French bistros that have become institutions in their own right.

Our chefs combine time-honored French techniques with locally sourced ingredients to create dishes that are both familiar and exciting. From our perfectly cooked steaks frites to our delicate pastries, every item on our menu tells a story of French culinary tradition.

We believe that dining is an experience to be savored, which is why we've created an atmosphere that transports you to the charming streets of Paris from the moment you step through our doors.
